SwiftFix Help Center

How to Create an Account?

Go to the registration page and enter your username, email and password. After submitting the form, your account will be created successfully.

How to Login?

Open the login page and enter your registered email or username with your password. After login you can access your dashboard.

How to Update Profile?

After logging in, open the profile page where you can update your personal details like phone number and date of birth.

Need More Help?

If you are facing any problem while using SwiftFix, please contact the admin or send a request through the support section.
